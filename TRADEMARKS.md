# WindowsGSH trademark and brand policy

The WindowsGSH name, logos, badge, artwork, visual identity, and associated branding identify the official WindowsGSH project and are reserved by their respective owner.

## Allowed descriptive use

You may use the plain-text name “WindowsGSH” when truthfully referring to the official project, linking to <https://windowsgsh.com>, reporting compatibility, or explaining that an independent module works with WindowsGSH.

Descriptive use must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, ownership, or official status.

## Permission required

Prior written permission is required to:

- use WindowsGSH in the name of another application, service, organisation, domain, package, or distributed fork;
- use or modify the WindowsGSH logo, badge, artwork, or distinctive visual identity outside an official WindowsGSH repository, except for qualifying community-module badge use expressly permitted below;
- present a fork or derivative as WindowsGSH, an official edition, or an approved continuation;
- use the branding in advertising, merchandise, paid services, or commercial promotion; or
- register a confusingly similar name, logo, account, domain, or mark.

Permission to view or modify source code does not grant trademark or branding rights.

## Community modules

Despite the general permission requirement above, an independent module may state “for WindowsGSH” or “compatible with WindowsGSH” and may use the unmodified approved WindowsGSH module badge without separate written permission when it:

- links to the official WindowsGSH website;
- does not imply that WindowsGSH created, reviewed, guarantees, or supports the module; and
- satisfies the [Community module requirements](https://github.com/WindowsGSH/WindowsGSH/wiki/Submitting-a-Community-Module).

The module's own name and branding must remain visually distinct from the WindowsGSH application.

The badge may not be used as an application icon, project logo, or evidence of endorsement. This limited permission ends automatically if the module stops satisfying the Community module requirements. All other logo, badge, artwork, and branding use requires prior written permission.

## Reporting misuse

Report misleading or unauthorised use through the official WindowsGSH repository. Do not include private personal information in a public report.
