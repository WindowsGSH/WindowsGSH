# WindowsGSH Code of Conduct

## Our standards

WindowsGSH aims to provide a welcoming, constructive community for users and
contributors of every background and experience level.

Expected behaviour includes:

- Treating other people with respect and patience.
- Giving and receiving technical feedback constructively.
- Focusing discussions on the project and the issue being addressed.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding the publication of personal information or
  credentials.
- Accepting reasonable moderation decisions and correcting harmful behaviour.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, sexualised content, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and sharing
another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in WindowsGSH repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, Wiki contributions, and other official project spaces. It also
applies when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
If no private contact method is available, use GitHub's reporting tools on the
relevant issue, comment, or account.
Security vulnerabilities should instead follow the separate
[Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

Project maintainers will review reports in confidence where reasonably
possible. They may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, lock
discussions, or ban participants when necessary. Deliberately false or abusive
reports may themselves result in moderation.

Enforcement decisions will consider the context, severity, recurrence, and
impact of the behaviour. Retaliation against someone who reports a genuine
concern is not permitted.
